abalone

abalone is a research assistant for your terminal that keeps your files and questions private.

It talks only to AI models running in verified secure hardware, and shows you exactly what leaves your computer before it does.

Your messages are encrypted for the model's secure hardware before they leave your computer.

Paste this into a terminal on macOS or Linux, in the folder you want to work in:


    
    

    
This gift also lists a Tor onion address, used only if the first address does not answer. It needs Tor installed (Tor Browser, or tor), with ABALONE_TOR_SOCKS set to its SOCKS port. You do not need it otherwise.
